# Google Meet through Vellarin Hub

Google Meet exposes 25 actions. A sample of real action names (use `find_app_actions` for the rest):

**Reads (run at once)**
- Get conference record by name
- Get Meet details
- Get Member
- Get Participant
- Get Participant Details
- Get Recording
- Get recordings by conference record ID
- Get Smart Note

**Writes (wait for the owner's approval, then run once)**
- Batch Update Google Meet Members
- Create Google Meet Space
- Create Member
- Delete Google Meet Space Member
- End active conference
- Update Google Meet Member
- Update Google Meet Space
